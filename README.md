Odd One Out
A real-time party game for 2–8 players. Everyone gets a secret prompt, except one player, who gets a slightly different prompt. Answer, compare, vote, and see who can blend in.
How to play
1. One player creates a room and shares the six-character room code.
2. Everyone joins from their own device.
3. Each player answers their private prompt.
4. Compare answers and vote for the player with the different prompt.
5. Play five rounds and see who wins.
A correct vote earns 1 point. The odd one out earns 2 points if they escape detection. The player with the most points after the final round wins.
Features
- Room-code multiplayer for 2–8 players
- Private prompts and answers, followed by group discussion and voting
- Live room updates across players’ devices
- Responsive interface with a late-night game-show look
- Serverless game sessions stored with Netlify Blobs
